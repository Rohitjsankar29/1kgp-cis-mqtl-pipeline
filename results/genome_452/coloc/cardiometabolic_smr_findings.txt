=====================================================

CAD (coronary, Aragam 2022): 22 loci - TBX20, MYL2 (cardiac), SMARCA4
T2D (DIAMANTE EUR): 23 loci - HNF1A (MODY3!), FGFR3, ABCC5, PIM3

Method: SMR v1.4.2 + HEIDI; mQTL (452 1000G-ONT, ASM-corroborated candidates) vs
disease GWAS; in-sample 452 LD; hg38 (GWAS lifted from b37). --diff-freq-prop 0.99
(reference-cohort AF differences expected).
Canonical disease genes recovered (HNF1A/diabetes, MYL2/TBX20/heart) validate approach.
